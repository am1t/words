[David started](https://forkingmad.blog/ten-pointless-facts-about-me/) the blogging challenge, but I saw [Pete post about this](https://pimoore.ca/posts/10-pointless-facts-about-me) first. Since then, I have seen many others do the same. So, here are my responses.

1. Do you floss your teeth?  
   Nope. Flossing teeth is not big in India.
2. Tea, coffee, or water?  
   Tea is love. Black coffee is what I drink currently.
3. Footwear preference?  
   Clogs. Prefer Crocs.
4. Favourite dessert?  
   [Ras malai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ras_malai). But then I am not a dessert person in general.
5. The first thing you do when you wake up?  
   Drink a glass of water.
6. Age you’d like to stick at?  
   Why would I do that? If I must say, 22 it is. I had just joined my first job – new city, new people. Plus the least responsibilities.
7. How many hats do you own?  
   Don't love hats. I do own a cap, though, if that counts.
8. Describe the last photo you took?  
   [Curious Bee](https://www.youtube.com/@curiousbee) bursting crackers – it is Diwali time.
9. Worst TV show?  
   Can there be just one? All the shows that I gave up watching.
10. As a child, what was your aspiration for adulthood?  
    Something to do with computers. I ended up doing just that.
