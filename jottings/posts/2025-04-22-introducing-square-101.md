Have you ever been listening to a podcast, like I was recently, and heard a guest casually say, "It's AI. It's all just 0s and 1s after all"? This time, that last statement made me pause. We use this phrase often, but do we ever ponder why that's the case? Why are they 0s and 1s? What's so special about binary?

It hit me that we encounter jargon like this all the time—in news reports, product launches, even ads—and most of the time, *we just nod along*. We accept these terms as truths without really questioning them.

That's precisely why I decided to call this newsletter "Square 101". It’s about going back to basics, to the starting point, like returning to the first square on a game board.

And I want to start with myself. I want to understand the terms I’ve been glossing over for years. So, this is my goal: to ask the questions you might be too busy (or too polite) to ask—and share what I learn along the way. Each issue will dive into one concept, *just enough to give you an awareness*, so next time you hear it, you won’t just let it slide by.

Honestly, this is a little selfish—I'm doing it to jumpstart my own learning. I’ve already got a six-month-long list of topics I want to explore, and I’m genuinely excited to get this going.
