I released a minor upgrade to the [Post Stats plugin](https://micro.blog/account/plugins/view/22), which fixes the incorrect count of posts with images. This release also introduces a setting to disable the year recap, removing the section from the stats page. This setting will be useful if you add the shortcode on a different page and don't want it included on the stats page. It will also allow you to remove the recap from the stats next year.

This completes the changes that I had initially planned.
