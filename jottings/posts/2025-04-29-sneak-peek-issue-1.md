I have scheduled the first issue of [Square 101](https://amitgawande.com/squareone) for delivery in two days, on May 1st. Here's a quick peek at what's coming. I would love for you to join me on the learning journey.

![](/images/square101-sneak-peek.png)

## What if I get it wrong?

I don't claim to be an expert—I’m learning by reading, reflecting, and gathering ideas from around the web. *I will be wrong. Probably a lot.* And that’s okay.

Square 101 is a work in progress—part writing, part thinking, part conversation. If you spot something off, I’d love to hear from you. I’ll happily share corrections in future issues with credit.

## What to expect?

The topics will mostly stay adjacent to things I am good at, such as tech. I’m not trying to cover every angle or go super deep. As I mentioned in the [introductory post](https://amitgawande.com/blog/2025/introducing-square-101), I want to dive into a topic just enough to raise your awareness, spark questions and maybe kickstart your curiosity. I won’t be adding reading lists or long reference sections — Wikipedia (and the internet) have that part covered.

**A few promises.** It’s free. It will have no ads. And it won’t land in your inbox every other day. I aim for the 1st and 15th of every month.
