I don't use AI to write. But I do use AI for review. I want to get my words, the way I frame the sentence, right. I don't make AI change what I have written - no rephrasing or changing the tone. Tone is mine. The soul is mine. AI cleans up if something is absolutely off. 

I use AI to code. I avoid the grind.

I don't consciously use AI to generate images. What's the point? I would rather not have an image if it is AI-generated. What about the photos I take? Aren't they already processed via AI? I don't care.
