---
title: On Memories
slug: on-memories
---
Memories continually strengthen the emotions one lives through. We remember ourselves being happier than we actually are. In the same way, we remember sad moments as far more painful than they actually were. This is also known as the peak-end rule.

> The peak–end rule is a psychological heuristic in which people judge an experience largely based on how they felt at its peak (i.e., its most intense point) and at its end, rather than based on the total sum or average of every moment of the experience

So I never relive the sad memories. Instead, I remind myself that it was a lot less painful than what I remember it being.
